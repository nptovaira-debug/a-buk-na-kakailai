Privacy Policy

A Buk na Kakailai · Kuanua Hymns

Effective date: 19 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how A Buk na Kakailai (“the App”) handles information when you use the Android app. The App is a digital hymn book containing Kuanua hymns. It is published under the developer name “A Buk na Kakailai”.

1. Information stored on your device

The App lets you save favourites, recently opened hymns, personal notes, service playlists and reading preferences such as text size and dark mode. These items are stored locally on your device using Android app storage. The App does not provide an account or synchronise these items to a server operated by the developer.

Personal notes that you enter are intended to remain on your device. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or its app data may be able to see them.

2. Advertising and third-party services

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner and native advertisements. When ads are requested or displayed, Google and its advertising partners may automatically receive or process information such as your IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app and device information, approximate location inferred from network information, ad impressions, interactions and diagnostic information. The information processed and the purposes may depend on your device settings, consent choices, ad configuration and Google's policies.

The developer does not receive or store your personal notes, favourites or service playlists through the App's advertising integration. Google processes advertising information under its own privacy terms. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Consent and privacy choices

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage consent where required. Depending on your location and applicable requirements, you may see a message asking for your advertising and data-use choices. Where the App provides a “Privacy choices” option, you can reopen the available privacy form to review or change your choices. Ads are initialised only when Google's consent information indicates that ad requests may be made.

For more information about Google's consent tools, see Google's UMP guidance for Android.

4. Information the developer collects

The App does not ask you to create an account, provide your name, email address, phone number or contacts, and the developer does not operate an App account database. The developer does not use the App to collect your personal notes or saved hymn preferences. Google and its service providers may process technical and advertising information as described above.

5. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored favourites, notes, history, playlists and preferences remain on your device until you remove them within the App (where that option is available), clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage removes locally stored App data from that device. The developer does not hold a server-side copy of this local information.

Advertising-related information handled by Google is subject to Google's retention practices and the settings and choices available through Google. Please refer to Google's privacy resources for details.

6. Security

The App stores its saved content in Android's app-specific local storage and does not transmit those notes or preferences to a developer-operated server. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Keep your device protected with a screen lock and do not share access to it if your saved notes are private.

7. Children's privacy

The App is a general hymn-reading tool and is not designed as a service for collecting personal information from children. Advertising content and eligibility are also subject to the applicable Google Play and Google advertising settings and policies. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer, contact us using the details below.

8. International users

The App may be used in different countries, including the United Kingdom and countries in the European Economic Area. Where required, Google consent messages are used to present relevant choices before advertising requests are made. Your rights may vary depending on the privacy laws that apply where you live.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App's features, advertising services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this webpage. The effective date above indicates when this version took effect.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer at nptovaira@gmail.com.
