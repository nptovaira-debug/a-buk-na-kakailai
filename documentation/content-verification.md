# Content verification policy

## Source edition

The working transcription is based on the 1960 *A Buk na Tinir, ma A Buk na Kakailai ure ra Lotu, ma A Lotu na Pupunang* (Kuanua, Blanche Bay Dialect), published by Methodist Mission Press, Rabaul, New Britain, T.N.G.

## Current transcription state

- 250 numbered hymn records are represented in the structured dataset.
- The supplied scan has missing printed pages 188–189.
- Hymns 237–239 are not reproduced in the supplied source. Their records are retained as empty placeholders; no lyrics have been invented.
- The transcription is OCR-assisted and is not yet a fully verified scholarly transcription.
- The `sourceReference` field preserves the reference string printed in the current web edition. It must not be mistaken for a scan page citation.

## Editorial rules

1. Preserve historical spelling, wording, punctuation, capitalization and stanza order.
2. Do not silently correct apparent OCR errors or modernize language.
3. Record proposed corrections separately with hymn number, exact original transcription, proposed reading, evidence, reviewer and date.
4. Only mark a correction verified after checking the original scan or a reliable copy of the 1960 edition.
5. Never fill missing pages or hymns by guessing or using unrelated hymn versions.
6. Keep the original transcription available even when a verified reading is later added.

## Validation performed during dataset extraction

The dataset was generated from the existing `index.html` hymn articles. A structural check found 250 numbered records (1–250), 27 section headings and 914 stanza blocks. Hymns 237–239 retain their displayed titles but have no stanza content in the source HTML; they remain empty records. These counts are structural checks only; they do not prove textual accuracy against the printed book.
